# Exact Steps to Scrape Yacht Charter Leads on Apollo.io

Apollo.io gives you 10,000 free email credits per month. We are going to filter their massive database to find the exact decision-makers (Founders/CEOs) of Yacht Charter companies who have the authority to say "yes" to a new website.

## 1. Set Up Apollo
1. Go to [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/) and create a free account.
2. Once logged in, click the **Search** tab at the top, and then click **People** (not Companies).

## 2. Apply the Golden Filters
On the left side of the screen, you will see a bunch of filters. Apply these exactly:

1. **Job Titles:** 
   - Click "Job Titles" and type in: `Founder`, `Owner`, `CEO`, `Managing Director`. 
   - Select them from the dropdown so they are all added.
2. **Location:** 
   - Enter high-net-worth coastal cities where yachting is huge.
   - E.g., `Miami, Florida`, `Fort Lauderdale, Florida`, `Dubai, UAE`, `Monaco`.
3. **Industry & Keywords:** 
   - Under Industry, select `Leisure, Travel & Tourism` or `Maritime`.
   - Under Keywords, type in: `"Yacht Charter"` or `"Boat Rental"`.
4. **Email Status:** 
   - *CRITICAL:* Scroll down to "Email Status" and check the box that says **"Verified"**. (If you don't do this, your emails will bounce and ruin your domain reputation).
5. **Employees:** 
   - Set to `1-10` or `11-50`. (You don't want massive cruise line companies; you want boutique luxury charters where the owner actually checks their own email).

## 3. Export the Leads
1. Apollo will now show you a list of perfect prospects matching those filters.
2. Select the top **50 people** using the checkboxes on the left.
3. Click the **Save** button at the top to save them to a list (e.g., "Miami Yacht Charters").
4. Go to the **Saved** tab at the top of Apollo.
5. Select all the people in your list, click the three dots (`...`), and select **Export to CSV**.

## 4. Send the Campaign
Open that CSV file. You now have a spreadsheet of 50 Verified First Names, Company Names, and Email Addresses.
