# The Yacht Charter "Teaser" Email (Email 1)

This is the very first email you will send to potential clients. 

**The Goal:** Do NOT sell a website. The goal is simply to get them to reply with the word "Yes". Once they say "Yes", our GitHub Action automatically generates their custom preview and emails it to them.

## The Template

**Subject:** Quick question regarding {{Business_Name}}'s website

Hi {{First_Name}},

I was looking into booking a charter in {{City}} this morning and came across {{Business_Name}}'s website. Your fleet looks incredible, but I noticed the mobile booking flow on your current site is quite outdated, which is likely causing high-end clients to bounce.

I actually spent a few hours yesterday building a high-converting, modern interactive prototype specifically for {{Business_Name}} to show you how top-tier charter companies are structuring their sites this year.

Would you be open to checking out the live preview? Just drop a quick "yes" and I'll send the link right over.

Best regards,

**[Your Name]**
Digital Growth Strategist

---

## Why this works for Yacht Charters:
1. **The Hook:** You compliment their fleet (yacht owners have big egos about their boats) but immediately point out a specific business flaw (mobile booking flow).
2. **The Value:** You spent "a few hours" working on something *specifically* for them before ever asking for money.
3. **The Low Friction Ask:** You aren't asking for a 30-minute phone call. You are just asking for permission to send a link. It takes them 1 second to reply "yes".
